What is Power Yoga?

Power Yoga is a high-energy workout combining strength-building poses, flowing movements, and a focus on endurance. It's perfect for those who want to enhance their fitness level while improving mental focus.

Benefits of Power Yoga

  • Improves cardiovascular health
  • Builds full-body strength
  • Boosts stamina and energy levels
  • Enhances mental focus and clarity
