Avocado Toast Delight

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of whole grain bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: Chili flakes, cherry tomatoes, or microgreens

Instructions

  • Toast the bread slices until golden brown.
  • Slice the avocado in half, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
  • Mash the avocado with a fork and mix in the lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  • Spread the avocado mixture evenly on the toasted bread slices.
  • Top with your choice of chili flakes, cherry tomatoes, or microgreens for extra flavor.
  • Serve immediately and enjoy!
