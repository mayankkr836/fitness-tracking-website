What is Pranayama?

Pranayama is the ancient practice of breath control. By harnessing the power of the breath, Pranayama helps to balance the body and mind, leading to improved health and well-being.

  • Enhances oxygen flow to the body
  • Reduces stress and anxiety
  • Increases mental clarity and focus
  • Improves lung capacity and respiratory function

Pranayama Breathing Techniques

Nadi Shodhana (Alternate Nostril Breathing)

This technique involves alternating the flow of breath through each nostril, balancing the left and right hemispheres of the brain and calming the nervous system.

Kapalbhati (Skull Shining Breath)

Kapalbhati is a forceful exhalation technique that cleanses the respiratory system, energizes the body, and enhances mental clarity.

Ujjayi (Victorious Breath)

This deep breathing technique creates a soothing sound, promoting relaxation and improving focus during yoga practice.
